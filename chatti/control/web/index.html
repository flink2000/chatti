    Getting Chatti onto your Wi-Fi

    1. On the Chatti, hold BOOT for 4 seconds — it opens a Wi-Fi network of its own.
    2. Connect a phone or PC to the network Chatti Server-…. The display shows the exact name.
    3. On a phone the setup page opens by itself. If it does not, open the address the display shows underneath.
    4. Pick your own Wi-Fi, enter the password. Leave the address field empty.
    5. Save. The Chatti restarts and finds the server on its own.

    If the display stays on “Connecting to …”, the Chatti has joined the Wi-Fi but got no address from the router — move it closer to the router, or restart the router.
    If it still has not found the server after a few minutes, enter the address in step 4 under “Advanced”: http://…:8003/xiaozhi/ota/
